# Legacy native-route boundary

Earlier internal workflows used CAD2022 COM helpers for CAD access, road constraints and background candidates. They are not exported here and are not the MCP-first default or a fallback for missing/refused tools.

pair_layout.py is pure geometry. Current verified tools supply native measurements, writes/readback and persistence. Authorized legacy/native adapters belong to project/backend code; this Skill neither supplies nor authorizes them.
